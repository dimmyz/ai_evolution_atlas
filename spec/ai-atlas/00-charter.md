# 00 — Project Charter

## Working title

**AI Evolution Atlas**

## Product purpose

Build a visually strong, source-grounded interactive website that helps a curious technical reader understand **how modern foundation AI evolved**, not merely which model came out when.

The site should answer questions such as:
- What changed after the Transformer architecture appeared?
- Which model families and research ideas led to later systems?
- Which organizations and people are connected to important milestones?
- How did the center of gravity move from scale -> instruction/chat -> open models -> multimodality/reasoning -> agents/tool use?
- What is a direct family relationship versus a broader influence?

## Audience

Primary:
- software/AI practitioners;
- technical managers and founders;
- students and educators;
- informed general readers.

Secondary:
- journalists/researchers needing a navigable starting map.

## Product promise

The user should be able to open the site and, within 30 seconds:
1. understand the time span and major eras;
2. select a milestone;
3. see why it mattered;
4. follow at least one lineage/relation;
5. open the source behind the claim.

## Non-goals

This is not:
- a live “best model” leaderboard;
- an exhaustive database of every AI model;
- a benchmark comparison engine;
- a company ranking site;
- a news feed;
- a claim that model history is a simple biological family tree.

## Factory purpose

Use a real information product to validate a stronger Hermes multi-agent flow after the Aquarium pilot:
- research lane is real and source-sensitive;
- architecture and data contract matter;
- independent Anthropic review is used;
- visual quality is a MUST rather than an afterthought;
- Playwright exists from day one;
- workspace contamination is mechanically rejected;
- Orchestrator supervises via bounded/event-driven checks rather than recursive polling.
